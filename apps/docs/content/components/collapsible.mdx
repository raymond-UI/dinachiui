## Agent

Inside an [`AgentProvider`](/docs/components/agent), Collapsible is listed as `disclosure` with `open`, under its trigger's name. It takes `open` and `close`, and what the panel shows is listed once it is open. The change goes through `onOpenChange`, where `isAgentChange(details)` is true and `details.cancel()` refuses it.

```ts
await agent.act({ steps: [{ ref: "Shipping details", action: "open" }] })
```
